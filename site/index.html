An honest boundary.

The extension reports availability; it does not manufacture access where a platform has not provided it.

It does

  • Read standard exposed caption tracks
  • Identify language and track kind
  • Flag machine or human captions when metadata says so
  • Keep a temporary, in-memory reader view

It never does

  • Extract hidden or restricted tracks
  • Download video or audio
  • Transcribe with AI
  • Bypass DRM or platform controls
