Privacy, kept plain.

Caption Source Check is designed to work locally in your browser. We do not collect, receive, sell, or retain the captions you read.

What the extension accesses

Only after you select the extension, it receives temporary access to the active tab. It reads standard caption-track information already exposed by that page: track labels, language, type, live status, and active caption cues. Browser-internal pages cannot be checked.

What stays on your device

The reader holds up to 150 recent cue lines in page memory so you can follow and briefly scroll back. That memory is discarded when the source page or reader closes. The extension uses no local or cloud storage.

Network and analytics

The extension makes no external network requests and includes no analytics, advertising, cookies, or third-party scripts. The website may be served with standard short-lived server logs by its host; it sets no tracking cookies.

Your choices

Close the reader or source tab to discard its temporary transcript. Remove the extension at any time through your browser’s extension settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project repository linked from the downloaded package.
